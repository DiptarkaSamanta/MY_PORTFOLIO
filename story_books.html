Cloud PDF Storage & Vault Integration

How to securely host, stream, and read your personal hard drive PDFs online

Architecture Guide & Vault Reader
1

Cloudflare R2 / Supabase Storage

Upload your PDF files from your hard drive to Cloudflare R2 or Supabase. They offer 10 GB+ free storage with zero egress download fees.

2

Secure Pre-Signed Token URLs

Generate temporary signed URLs (valid for e.g. 60 mins). This keeps your private PDFs 100% secure from unauthorized downloads and public scrapers.

3

In-App PDF Viewer Modal

Click on any story card to read the PDF directly inside our glassmorphic reader modal without leaving your portfolio app!
